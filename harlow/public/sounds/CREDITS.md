# Sound credits

## door.mp3

Made from two recordings in **RPG Audio** by Kenney (https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio):
`doorOpen_1.ogg` (trimmed to 0.65 s) with `doorClose_1.ogg` laid over it from 0.48 s,
mixed to mono, limited and encoded as 80 kbps MP3 (1.2 s) with ffmpeg.
